Regulatory Note

LexFlow is a B2B practice-management platform — the same category as practice-management software used by law firms worldwide. Subscription and seat fees are charged to the firm, not the end-client. In keeping with Bar Council of India Rule 36 (Advocates Act, 1961), LexFlow does not rank or rate advocates, display star ratings in firm search results, or charge referral commissions tied to case value. The firm-discovery directory is a plain, factual listing (name, practice areas, courts, years established). These design choices are intentional and have been reviewed against current BCI enforcement guidance.
